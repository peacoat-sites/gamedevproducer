Sales per review Estimated units Gross at full price Net at full price

Published ratios mostly fall between about 30 and 80 sales per review, lower for small and recent games. Gamalytic data found about 36 for games with under 100 reviews and about 53 for games with 1,000 to 10,000. Full-price revenue is an upper bound: discounts, regional pricing and bundles mean real revenue is lower.

Net is after Valve's 30% share (25% above $10M and 20% above $50M in lifetime revenue, not modeled here) and an 8% refund allowance, before sales tax, regional pricing and any publisher split.
