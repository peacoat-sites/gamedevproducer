Typical uses GameDiscoverCo's median first-week ratio: 0.10 sales per wishlist for games priced above $10, and 0.15 at $10 or less. Weak and strong launches are half and double the median. Early Access is about a third lower. Month one is about 1.25x week one, and year one is the median 2.46x week-one revenue. Net is after Valve's 30% share and an 8% refund allowance, before sales tax, regional pricing and any publisher split. Free-to-play games are not modeled, because wishlists convert to downloads rather than sales.
