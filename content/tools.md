Most of the work of production is turning uncertainty into decisions: when to ship, what to cut, what it will cost and whether the numbers work. These free tools do the arithmetic and the bookkeeping so you can spend your time on the decisions. Every one runs in the browser, explains its assumptions, and needs no sign-up.

## How the tools fit together

A typical path through a project uses them in order. Start with the [schedule planner](/game-development-schedule-planner/) to turn a target date into milestones, and the [budget calculator](/indie-game-budget-calculator/) to see what that plan costs. Look for funding in the [grants database](/game-development-grants/). Before launch, use the [wishlist calculator](/steam-wishlist-to-sales/) and the [Steam calendar](/steam-sale-dates/) to pick a realistic launch window, and the [certification checklist](/console-certification-checklist/) if you ship on console. Keep the [risk register](/downloads/game-risk-register-template.xlsx) open the whole way through.

For the methods behind the tools, start with the [game production guide](/game-production-guide/).
