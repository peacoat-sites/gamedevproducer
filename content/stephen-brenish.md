Stephen Brenish is a Lead Game Producer at Epic Games, where he oversees Fortnite and Unreal Engine feature development and manages producers and engineers across the Fortnite Gameplay/Online team. Across 14+ years in game production he has shipped and run live titles at scale, previously as a Senior Program Manager at Blizzard Entertainment and in product roles at Facebook. A Certified ScrumMaster, he founded GameDevProducer as a working handbook of the production frameworks, milestone planning, and live-ops practices he uses every day.
