Game production has a vocabulary of its own, and much of it means slightly different things at different studios. This glossary defines 103 terms producers use every day, grouped by where they come up: the phases and milestones of a project, planning, agile process, risk and quality, launch and certification, live operations, business and contracts, roles, and pipelines.

<!--more-->

Definitions are short on purpose: enough to unblock you in a meeting, with a link to the full guide or tool where there is one. Where studios genuinely disagree, such as exactly what "alpha" means, the definition says so. Search the list, or jump to a section.
