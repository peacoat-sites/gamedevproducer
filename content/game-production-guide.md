Game production is the work of turning a creative vision into a shipped, and often still-running, game: deciding what to build, in what order, with whom, by when and for how much, and then keeping all of that honest as reality arrives. This guide organizes everything on GameDevProducer into the order you need it, from the first pitch to running a live game, with the tools that do the arithmetic along the way.

<!--more-->

Each section starts with the ideas that matter most, then points to the deeper guides. If you are new to production, read it top to bottom. If you are in the middle of a project, jump to the stage you are in.

## 1. What producers actually do

Producers own the "how" and the "when" of a game, not the "what". The job is part planner, part translator between disciplines, part risk manager and part early-warning system. The best producers make decisions easier for everyone else.

## 3. Planning: scope, schedule and budget
